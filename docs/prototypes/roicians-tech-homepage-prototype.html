Prototype review notes

What is approved, and what is sample

Approved

  • Brand: ROICIANS TECH, with the line "Practical technology training for the AI era".
  • The ten Program names and the two initial display groups.
  • Button labels: Talk to an Advisor, Explore Programs, Join Interest List.
  • "Next Cohort: Coming Soon" when a Program has no published upcoming Batch.
  • No prices, fees, seat counts or capacity anywhere public.
  • Light palette (owner decision, 2026-10-22):
    #FFFFFF #F1F6FF #2347D0 #14263F

Sample or placeholder

  • All headlines, descriptions and offer text marked Sample.
  • The wordmark: the authorized logo files will replace it.
  • Footer legal links: wording needs business and legal approval.
  • Links in this prototype only jump within the page.

Not shown on purpose

  • No testimonials, student counts, employer logos, placement results or dates (none approved).
  • No dark-navy section; Corporate Training uses the soft blue band.
  • No stock photography, AI robots, glow or heavy gradients.
  • Admin login is not promoted on the public site.
