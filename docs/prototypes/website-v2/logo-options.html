ROICIANS TECH logo lockups, revision 2

Two modernised ways to add TECH to the original ROICIANS logo, shown side by side for your choice. The previous header and footer treatments are withdrawn. The original symbol and wordmark are unchanged.

What changed from the rejected version

  • No vertical divider, no horizontal rules and no underline.
  • TECH is set in Instrument Sans SemiBold with minimal letter spacing (4–6 %), not widely spaced capitals.
  • TECH is sized and placed from the wordmark itself: on its baseline (Option 1) or flush with the end of its S (Option 2). This makes the two words read as one name.
  • One arrangement is used in the header, footer and on phones; only the size changes.
  • The tagline stays outside the logo, as supporting footer copy.

1. The two options

Large size first, then at header size in navy (the same colour as the logo) and with a cobalt accent, on white and on soft blue.

Option 1

TECH alongside the wordmark

A smaller TECH follows ROICIANS on the same baseline, so the name reads in one line: ROICIANS TECH.

Option 2

TECH beneath the end of the wordmark

A smaller TECH sits under the end of ROICIANS, its right edge flush with the S, forming a compact two-line name block.

2. Website header (desktop, actual size)

The real 80 px header height. Grey bars stand in for the menu, Log in and the call-to-action button.

3. Footer (actual size)

The same arrangement as the header, larger, with the tagline as separate copy beneath it. The live footer uses the soft-blue background.

4. Phone (390 px wide)

The header bar on white and the footer on soft blue, with the real Log in button and menu button sizes and a 16 px side margin.

Recommendation

Option 1, with TECH in the same navy as the logo

TECH
TECH
  • One arrangement everywhere: symbol, ROICIANS, TECH, in one line, in the header, the footer and on phones. Only the size changes. Sizes (symbol / wordmark height): header 50 / 22 px; phone header 40 / 17 px; footer 68 / 30 px (50 / 22 px on phones).
  • Why Option 1: it reads as one name, ROICIANS TECH, in natural order; TECH stays legible on a phone; and the header stays slim.
  • Why navy rather than cobalt: a single colour keeps ROICIANS and TECH together as one identity. A cobalt TECH is fresh, but it makes TECH look like a separate label. Cobalt stays for buttons and links.
  • Footer: the logo, then the tagline "Practical technology training for the AI era" as ordinary text beneath it, not part of the logo.
  • Nothing is applied yet. The prototype's header and footer still show the withdrawn treatment until you choose. Once you choose, only the header and footer logo change.

How the original logo is preserved

  • Master file unchanged: docs/brand/roicians-logo-original.png, 433 × 370 transparent PNG, SHA-256 13ecd0b8ae36b968….
  • Symbol and wordmark: roicians-symbol.png and roicians-wordmark.png are straight crops of the original. Their pixels and colour are not changed. Each is scaled evenly by the browser, never stretched.
  • TECH: live text in Instrument Sans SemiBold (Inter as fallback), letter spacing 0.04em (Option 1) or 0.06em (Option 2). The colour is the original logo navy #2E496C, with cobalt #2347D0 shown only as an alternative.
  • Alignment: TECH's size and position come from measurements of the wordmark (cap height 39 of 57 px; baseline 43 px from the top; the S ends 5 px from the right edge). Alignment therefore holds at every size.
  • Backgrounds: white or soft blue only, never dark.
  • Resolution: the supplied PNG is small. It is sharp at header and footer sizes. A vector (SVG) or larger master is recommended before launch, and before using the logo larger than about 40 px wordmark height.
