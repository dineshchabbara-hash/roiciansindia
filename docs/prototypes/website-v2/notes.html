Prototype review notes

What is approved, what is sample, which actions are prototype-only, and where every image comes from.

Approved content used

  • The public brand name ROICIANS TECH, the line "Practical technology training for the AI era" and the original ROICIANS logo (supplied by the owner).
  • The ten Program names and the two initial groups: Career & Technology Programs, Specialist & Professional Programs.
  • Button labels Talk to an Advisor, Explore Programs and Join Interest List, and the state "Next Cohort: Coming Soon".
  • Online-first delivery with room for in-person or hybrid formats.
  • Rules: no public prices, fees, seat counts, capacity or private Batch details; no invented Batch dates.
  • Colour palette: white #FFFFFF, soft blue #F1F6FF, cobalt #2347D0, dark navy #14263F, plus the original logo blue #2E496C as a supporting colour.

Owner decisions so far (provisional, 2026-10-22)

These approve the branding direction provisionally. They are not final approval of the whole visual prototype.

  • The original logo artwork and navy colour are preserved: no redesign or recolouring.
  • Website logo (decision, 2026-10-22): the original ROICIANS logo without TECH. The header uses the original R symbol and wordmark side by side. The footer uses the original stacked logo, with the tagline as normal text beneath it. The R symbol alone is the favicon. See Website logo.
  • This is a website logo decision only. The legal company name stays Roicians Tech Pvt. Ltd., and other branding decisions are unchanged. The earlier TECH lockups (header Option B, footer Option A and the two revision-2 options) are no longer used; they remain in the review history.
  • Palette stays white, soft blue and cobalt.
  • The original topic-specific illustrations stay on the Program cards in the prototype.
  • For the live website: a limited amount of properly licensed professional photography in the hero and Corporate Training sections (not yet sourced).
  • "Start with a program in demand" is replaced by "Explore our technology programs". A demand claim needs independent substantiation before it can be used.

Sample copy (not approved launch content)

  • All headlines, slide text, value-proposition text, Program descriptions, group descriptions, Corporate Training and For Institutions text.
  • Featured Programs on the homepage (testing, data analytics, cybersecurity, generative AI and LLMOps) are a provisional choice for layout only, not an approved order.
  • "GenAI & LLMOps for GCC Teams" is the design's example Corporate Training audience.
  • Section headings such as "Explore our technology programs", "Training shaped around your teams" and "Not sure which program fits?" are sample wording.
  • Statements that would need evidence before launch: any claim about demand, outcomes, placement, employers or numbers. None are used in this prototype.

Prototype-only actions

  • Explore Program, About, Student and Trainer login, certificate verification, Privacy and Terms show a short note instead of navigating.
  • Talk to an Advisor scrolls to the enquiry section. Its button shows a note that nothing is sent; no submission is simulated.
  • Join Interest List shows a note; the form arrives with the enquiry pages (Phase 22H).
  • Working: navigation, the Programs page and its group filter, the hero slider (arrows, indicators, keyboard arrows, swipe), the Featured Programs carousel, the mobile menu and the Log in menu.

Images: sources and licensing

No stock photography or third-party images are used. Every image is an original illustration generated for this prototype from basic shapes by the script gen_illustrations.py, which is kept with the prototype files. There are no real people, client logos or AI robots. Silhouettes in the team illustrations are generic and do not depict actual ROICIANS clients, instructors or students.
